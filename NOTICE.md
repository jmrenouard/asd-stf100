# Legal Notice, Licensing, and Non-Affiliation

## Non-Affiliation and Trademark Recognition

`ASD-STF100` (*Français Technique Simplifié*) is an independent, open-source
controlled French technical writing specification, linter, and documentation
toolkit created for French-language procedures, operational runbooks, and safety
documentation.

It is inspired structurally by the controlled-language methodology of
`ASD-STE100` (Issue 9, 2025-01-15), while remaining completely independent in
language, vocabulary, rules adaptation, and examples.

  registered trade mark owned by ASD (**EU Trade Mark No. 017966390**; prior

  approved meanings, or official examples from `ASD-STE100`.
- For formal `ASD-STE100` English compliance, consult the official ASD
  specification at <https://www.asd-ste100.org>.
